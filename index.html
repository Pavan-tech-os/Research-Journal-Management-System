ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Simplifying the Research Publication Process

The Research Journal Management System provides a centralized platform for managing research papers from submission to publication.

Authors can submit papers and track revisions, editors can manage submissions and assign reviewers, while reviewers can provide feedback and recommendations.
